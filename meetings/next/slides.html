Background · 2 of 6

Hand over a model answer, or correct the student's own homework

Forward KL / SFT · the student copies teacher text

On-policy / reverse KL · the teacher grades the student's own attempt

L(θ) =  Ey ∼ πS  1⁄|y| Σt  KL( πS(· | x, y<t) ‖ πT(· | x, y<t) )
y ∼ πS  the trajectory is sampled from the student, for question x
KL( πS ‖ πT )  at position t, how far the student's next-token distribution is from the teacher's
Σt / |y|  averaged over every token of the attempt: each one is graded
reverse KL  mode-seeking: the student is pulled onto the teacher's modes, not made to cover all of them

GKD, Agarwal et al. 2023 (arXiv 2306.13649); Thinking Machines, "On-Policy Distillation", 2025. The trainer we run is TRL GKD with beta = 1: pure reverse KL on the provided trajectories.

Grading is expensive, so what gets graded is the design

Every graded token is one teacher forward pass

Two places to spend the judgement · question selection, then trajectory selection

Motivation. OPD is a training strategy. Its teacher is only as useful as the attempts and questions it is asked to judge. We are designing the part that decides what to grade.

Active On-Policy Distillation · Background04 / 06 · Definitions
Background · 4 of 6

Ten words, used the same way on every slide

questionOne math problem from the training pool (OpenThoughts) or an eval set. What gets labelled, selected and trained on.
training stepOne optimizer update on 32 trajectories (the effective batch). The unit of budget and the x-axis of every plot.
trajectoryOne sampled student completion for one question: the token sequence and its metadata (lengths, sampling settings, grade).
refreshEvery 10 training steps: evaluate the current weights, and roll out the next 10 steps' questions from those weights so training stays on-policy.
rolloutThe act of sampling trajectories from a model for a set of questions. One question yields num_rollouts trajectories.
strict correctnessA trajectory is correct only if a \boxed{} answer is present and Math-Verify accepts it. Cap-hit with no boxed answer is wrong, everywhere.
question selectionWhich questions receive teacher effort and enter a training set at all.
avg@4 / pass@4avg@4: mean accuracy over 4 attempts per eval question, the primary metric. pass@4: at least one of the 4 correct, the diversity monitor.
trajectory selectionFor a question that is in, which of its own rollouts are trained on (e.g. kl_mid: the middle-KL third of 12 rollouts).
correctness bucketQuestion label from teacher and student strict correctness: correct = at least 3 of 4 rollouts, wrong = at most 1 of 4. Buckets TC/SW, TC/SC, TW/SW, Mixed.

Student Qwen3.5-2B, teacher Qwen3.5-9B (frozen). Sampling everywhere: temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 20; cap 8192 new tokens.

Regime, every run

cap 8192 for labels, training and eval; strict scoring; effective batch 32; 100 training steps per arm = 3,200 trajectories.

Refresh, every 10 training steps

MATH-500 avg@4 + pass@4 on all 500; AIME 2025+2026 avg@16 + pass@16 on all 60. Then re-roll the next questions from the current weights.

loss, top-16 overlap ratio, overlap-token advantage, mean |H_S − H_T| on the training batch.
